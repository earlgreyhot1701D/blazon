## Brand & Style

This design system translates the ceremonial opulence and scholarly rigor of medieval illuminated armorials into a modern digital interface. It marries the tangible gravitas of historic heraldry—vellum substrates, gilded leaf borders, and wax seals—with responsive digital usability.

The target audience comprises genealogists, historians, antiquarian collectors, game masters, and prestige heritage brand consumers. The visual identity should evoke reverence, scholarly discovery, and artisanal craftsmanship.

### Aesthetic Foundation
- **Tactile Historicism & Skeuomorphism:** Elements simulate natural physical parchment, gilded illuminations, and wax seals through deliberate gradients, warm drop shadows, and ornamental brackets.
- **Hierarchical Formalism:** Balanced, axial, shield-centric layouts anchored by classical heraldic compositional balance.
- **Architectural Framing:** Structural cards and panels are treated as codex folios, using ornamental corner filigree, inset double borders, and rubricated accents.

## Colors

The palette is derived from medieval heraldic tinctures (Metals, Colors, and Stains), balanced against rich velvet grounds and archival vellum.

### Color Roles & Tinctures
- **The Ground (Oxblood Velvet):** Primary surface depth. `#22080E` serves as the canvas backdrop, while `#3C111B` forms structural container layers.
- **The Metal (Burnished Gold / Or):** Secondary key. `#C69C43` provides structural borders and emblems, highlighted with `#E5BE64` for illuminated focal points and grounded with `#7D5D1C` for etched depth.
- **The Substrate (Antique Vellum Parchment):** Neutral tone. `#F5EEDC` (high parchment), `#E8DCC2` (standard folio), and `#D4C3A3` (aged crease/border) provide tactile contrast against oxblood grounds.
- **Heraldic Accents (Tinctures):**
  - **Gules (Crimson Enamel):** `#8C1823` for primary alerts, rubrication, and wax seals.
  - **Azure (Lapis Lazuli):** `#1B3B5F` for archival links, reference tags, and secondary tinctures.
  - **Vert (Verdigris):** `#244B36` for confirmed validations and ecological blazon attributes.
  - **Purpure (Imperial Amethyst):** `#4A2040` for royal lineage markers.
  - **Sable (Iron Gall Ink):** `#141010` for high-contrast typography on vellum backgrounds.

## Typography

The typography unites classical epigraphic authority with archival record-keeping precision.

### Typographic Hierarchy & Character
- **Headlines (EB Garamond):** Used in all major headers, titles, and blazon titular inscriptions. Display headings leverage classical capitalizations, small caps where appropriate, and letter-spaced grandeur reminiscent of gilded stone carvings and rubricated folio headers.
- **Body & Editorial (Newsreader):** Carries the narrative text, heraldic descriptions, and historical lore. Its scholarly, warm serifs and generous x-height ensure high legibility across long-form reading on digital parchments.
- **Metadata & Archival Records (Space Mono):** Used for roll indices, catalogue numbers, lineage dating, tincture codes, and tabular arms classifications. The monospaced cadence brings the functional aesthetic of rare manuscript registry archives.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a balanced 12-column responsive grid modeled after traditional manuscript illuminations: wide exterior margins, balanced vertical axes, and dedicated framing stages.

### Breakpoints & Adaptive Rhythm
- **Desktop (12 Columns, 1280px+):** Centered presentation with wide outer gutters (`margin: 3rem`). Accommodates the central shield stage alongside paired flanking blazon scrolls and tincture legend panels.
- **Tablet (8 Columns, 768px – 1279px):** Columns collapse symmetrically. Shield stage retains central focal prominence with blazon metadata wrapping directly beneath.
- **Mobile (4 Columns, up to 767px):** Single-column vertical reflow (`margin-mobile: 1.25rem`, `gutter-mobile: 1rem`). Visual hierarchy prioritizes: (1) Shield crest, (2) Parchment blazon scroll with wax seal anchor, and (3) Archival metadata drawer.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through physical material layering rather than sterile gray drop shadows:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - **Velvet Ground (Base):** Deep `#22080E` matte void.
  - **Folio Platters (Tier 1):** `#3C111B` with subtle warm inner vignettes (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 30px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`).
  - **Parchment Cards & Scrolls (Tier 2):** `#F5EEDC` illuminated sheets with soft, warm-tinted atmospheric falloff (`0 12px 32px -4px rgba(20, 8, 12, 0.55)`).
  - **Shield Stage & Wax Seals (Tier 3):** Highest focal elevation, casting directional warm ambient shadows (`0 16px 40px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65)`) with gold specular edge definition (`0 0 0 1px #E5BE64`).
- **Gilded Borders & Corner Filigree:** High-tier containers feature double-line borders (`2px solid #C69C43` with an inset `1px solid rgba(198, 156, 67, 0.4)`) punctuated by ornamental brass-style corner brackets.

## Shapes

The geometric vernacular uses a disciplined `Soft` roundedness profile (`roundedness: 1`), keeping folios and shields rooted in architectural precision rather than contemporary bubbly forms.

- **Folios and Manuscript Cards:** Controlled 0.25rem corner radii (`space-xs`) adorned with sharp, stylized brass corner brackets.
- **Heraldic Escutcheons:** Custom curvilinear shield shapes (Heater, French, Kite, and Cartouche) anchored centrally in dedicated stages.
- **Interactive Controls:** Interactive action buttons break the strict rectangular mold by adopting pill silhouettes (`rounded-full`) to invite touch and indicate high clickability without conflicting with card geometry.

## Components

### Buttons & Action Controls
- **Primary Gilded Pill:** Burnished gold background (`#C69C43` to `#7D5D1C` linear gradient) with iron-gall ink text (`#141010`), full pill rounding, and subtle inset top highlights. Hover state intensifies with an outer `#E5BE64` radiance glow.
- **Secondary Folio Pill:** Dark oxblood ground (`#3C111B`) encased in a `1px` solid gold border (`#C69C43`) with antique vellum text (`#F5EEDC`).
- **Wax Seal Action Button:** Circular crimson lacquer button (`#8C1823`) with an embossed stamp emblem, raised beveled edges, and tactile press displacement on `:active`.

### Heraldic Cards & Folio Containers
- Bound within oxblood or vellum parchment backgrounds.
- Feature ornamental corner brackets rendered in burnished gold.
- Edges defined by an inner and outer double hairline border separated by `3px` spacing.

### The Blazon Scroll
- A textured parchment container (`#F5EEDC`) featuring curled top and bottom roll margins.
- Contains the official heraldic blazon rendered in `Newsreader` italic, sealed at the base by a crimson wax seal component that serves as a cryptographic verification status chip.

### Chips & Badges
- Compact pill-shaped status indicators with a `Space Mono` uppercase typeface.
- Bordered in `1px` metallic tincture wires corresponding to Or, Argent, Azure, or Gules, paired with low-opacity tincture background fills (`15%` opacity).

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Textured inset vellum troughs (`#E8DCC2`) with dark archival text (`#141010`).
- Deep inset box-shadow (`inset 0 2px 4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.25)`). Focus states display an illuminated gold hairline ring (`#C69C43`) and rubricated crimson caret.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square (checkbox) and diamond (radio) gold-wire frames (`#C69C43`).
- Checked states display a gilded Maltese cross or central heraldic fleur-de-lis pip.

### Central Shield Stage
- An elevated, focal canvas stage designed to isolate and showcase the coat of arms.
- Surrounded by an atmospheric radial glow emanating from behind the escutcheon, framed by symmetrical heraldic mantle flourishes.
